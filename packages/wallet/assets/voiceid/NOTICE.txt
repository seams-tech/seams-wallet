VoiceID speaker model

This deployment uses an ONNX export of SpeechBrain spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb.
Upstream: https://huggingface.co/speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb
Revision: 0f99f2d0ebe89ac095bcc5903c4dd8f72b367286
License: Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)

The model was converted to accept complete 16 kHz mono waveforms and emit
192-dimensional speaker embeddings. Preprocessing and model weights are local.
No model training or user recordings are included in this asset directory.
